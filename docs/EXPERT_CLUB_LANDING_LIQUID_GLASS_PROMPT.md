# Expert Club Landing — Liquid Glass Art Direction

Transform the Expert Club public landing page into a premium fitness-tech experience using the Bloom/liquid-glass reference as visual language, not as a literal template.

## Goal

Create a cinematic, high-end, conversion-focused landing page that feels like a real product cockpit for training, diet, check-ins, hydration, progress, challenges, and community.

## Visual System

- Dark premium base with controlled atmospheric depth.
- Liquid-glass surfaces with two tiers: light glass for pills/secondary cards and strong glass for hero shell, feature panels, and pricing.
- Keep Expert Club accents: Lime `#B7FF3C`, Sky `#5DDCFF`, Purple `#8B5CF6`.
- Use Poppins for landing display/body and Source Serif 4 only for italic emphasis inside hero/section headings.
- Preserve product clarity: no grayscale-only treatment, no floral identity, no Bloom copy.

## Hero

- Full-screen split composition on desktop: 52% strong-glass content panel and 48% atmospheric visual/product panel.
- Fullscreen autoplay, muted, looping video background with dark overlays and brand glows.
- Hero must immediately communicate: Expert Club, premium fitness, method, technology, and action.
- CTA treatment: rounded-full liquid-glass primary, lime action color, hover scale, active press state.

## Landing Sections

- Replace generic Tailwind card grids with layered panels and product-facing surfaces.
- Prioritize sections: Method, Inside the App, Before/After, Founder Plan, FAQ, Final CTA.
- Product copy should be direct, premium, and practical: "open the app and know what to do today."

## Motion

- Use subtle reveal, opacity, and translate motion.
- Hover scale should stay around `1.02` for cards and `1.05` for CTAs.
- Active scale around `0.95`.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Responsive

- Mobile becomes a vertical hero with stronger overlay, stacked CTAs, visible brand mark, and no desktop-only right panel.
- Maintain minimum 44px interactive targets.
- Avoid horizontal overflow at 375, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1280, and 1440 widths.

## Reject From Bloom

- Do not copy Bloom branding, logo, wording, flower/product context, or exact layout content.
- Do not force grayscale-only.
- Do not make a purely conceptual hero that hides the fitness product.
- Do not add glass everywhere if it damages readability.
